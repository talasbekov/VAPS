When Bratan asks "а зачем нам нужен X" or "а почему не объединить" mid-discussion — that's not confusion, that's strategic re-framing. He does this when he senses we're optimizing inside a frame that itself is wrong.

**Why:** During the 2026-05-31 brainstorming session for VAPS, mid-analysis of axis 1 (source of truth) he asked "why do we even keep PS and VX separate if КУ will provide statuses + org tomorrow?" — and he was right to ask. We were drilling into integration details for a separation that may itself be unnecessary.

**How to apply:** When he challenges a premise, STOP and validate the premise before continuing. Don't try to answer the original question — answer the meta-question first. Reframe the analysis around his question instead of treating it as a tangent.
